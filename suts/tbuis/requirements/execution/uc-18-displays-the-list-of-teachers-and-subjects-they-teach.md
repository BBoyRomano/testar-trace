# Displays the list of teachers and subjects they teach
ID: UC.18
Actor: teacher

## Preconditions
- Teacher is already logged in to the UIS

## Main flow
1. Teacher clicks on the option of the menu List of All Teachers

## Alternative flow
1. 1a. There is no subject in the UIS
2. Postcondition: Column of subjects is empty

## Postconditions
- Table List of All Teachers is displayed
- No data are overwritten or saved - no changes occur
